# User Research Synthesis Skill - Complete Content

This skill provides a structured methodology for transforming user research data into actionable insights. Here's the comprehensive framework:

## Core Components

**Data Collection Overview**: Documents research type, participant profiles, research questions, and methodology.

**Key Themes Identification**: Organizes findings into 4-8 major themes, each with description, prevalence data, supporting quotes, and product implications.

**Pain Points Analysis**: Categorizes issues by severity (High/Medium/Low), documents current workarounds, and provides specific evidence.

**Feature Requests**: Prioritizes requests as Must-Have (critical needs), High Value (significant improvements), or Nice-to-Have (incremental enhancements).

**User Workflow Insights**: Maps current state, pain points, ideal state, and opportunities for value addition.

**Segmentation Insights**: Identifies distinct user segments with unique characteristics and needs.

**Competitive Insights**: Documents competitor/alternative usage patterns, strengths, gaps, and switching barriers.

**Recommendations**: Provides prioritized suggestions with supporting evidence and expected impact.

## Quality Standards

The framework emphasizes identifying **patterns across participants** rather than listing individual comments. As the guide states: "Good synthesis identifies patterns, not just individual responses" and "connects insights to product decisions" with supporting evidence.

The methodology includes a complete template structure for deliverables, example theme documentation, and guidelines for analyzing interviews, quotes, and themes effectively.
