# Pricing Strategy Skill - Complete Content

## Overview
This skill guides pricing decisions for SaaS and digital products through structured frameworks covering customer segmentation, value metrics, competitive positioning, and packaging design.

## Core Framework
The skill establishes three foundational questions: identifying buyers, quantifying delivered value, and selecting a pricing model. It then presents six pricing models (per seat, usage-based, flat rate, tiered, freemium, value-based) with corresponding use cases and risks.

## Key Sections

**Freemium Guidelines:** The framework recommends freemium when "marginal cost per free user is near zero" and "product is inherently viral," with typical free-to-paid conversion benchmarks of 2–5%.

**Tiering Structure:** A recommended three-tier approach targets individuals (free/starter), SMBs (pro/growth), and enterprises (business), with price jumps of 3–5x between tiers considered normal.

**Competitive Positioning:** Three strategies are outlined—premium (20–40% above market), parity (matching market leaders), or value (below market, requiring strong unit economics).

## Practical Recommendations
- Price based on customer value, not production costs
- Include annual pricing with 15–20% discounts to improve cash flow
- Monitor conversion rates, churn, ARPU, and expansion revenue post-launch
- A/B test price changes where feasible
- For enterprise "contact us" pricing, include a price floor to qualify leads

The skill emphasizes that upgrade triggers should feel natural within the product rather than artificially manufactured.
