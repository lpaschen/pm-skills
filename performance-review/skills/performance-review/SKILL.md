# Performance Review Skill

This skill transforms rough notes into professionally written performance reviews ready for submission. It handles self-assessments, manager reviews, peer feedback, and 360 evaluations.

## Key Requirements

The skill asks users for: review type, period, person's name, role, achievements, growth areas, projects, company values (optional), and overall rating if needed.

## Output Format

Reviews follow a structured template including:

- **Overall Summary:** "High-level characterisation of the period. Acknowledge standout contributions."
- **Achievements & Impact:** Specific accomplishments with measurable outcomes
- **Strengths Demonstrated:** "3–4 bullet points. Each bullet = one strength, with one concrete example"
- **Areas for Growth:** Constructive development opportunities with suggested actions
- **Development Goals:** Clear, outcome-oriented objectives for the next period
- **Competency Ratings:** Optional framework-based assessment table
- **Closing Recommendation:** Summary assessment and next steps

## Core Principles

The skill emphasizes avoiding vague language like "strong communicator" without supporting evidence. All statements require concrete examples. Growth areas must be honest yet constructive. Goals should be measurable with clear success criteria.

The quality checklist ensures every achievement has specific outcomes, strengths include examples, and tone remains professional and fair throughout.
