# Retention Analysis Skill — Complete Content

## Overview
This skill structures retention analysis, churn investigations, and engagement deep-dives for product teams. It triggers on requests about user retention, churn, DAU/MAU metrics, or why users are leaving.

## Key Framework Components

**Retention Curve Analysis:**
The skill distinguishes between two critical factors: "(1) Steepness of initial drop (D1–D7) — onboarding problem" and "(2) Long-term floor level — product-market fit indicator."

**Six Core Metrics:**
- D1, D7, and D30 retention rates
- DAU/MAU ratio (stickiness indicator)
- Churn rate
- Net Revenue Retention

**Four-Step Investigation Process:**
1. Segment users by cohort (new vs. returning, paid vs. free, acquisition channel, onboarding completion, feature usage)
2. Identify inflection points where drops occur
3. Find "aha moment" correlations predicting long-term retention
4. Conduct qualitative churn interviews

## Output Template
Structured analysis includes current snapshot against benchmarks, retention curve assessment, PMF signals, root cause hypotheses with evidence and confidence levels, aha moment correlations, and prioritized interventions with effort estimates and monitoring plans.

## Critical Guidelines
- Avoid generic recommendations; specify exact changes and rationale
- Benchmark against industry peers
- Recognize when low DAU/MAU (<5%) signals PMF issues, not tactical retention problems
- Prioritize direct user interviews alongside quantitative analysis
