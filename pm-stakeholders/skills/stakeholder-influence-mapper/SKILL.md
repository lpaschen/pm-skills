# Stakeholder Influence Mapper Skill - Complete Content

## Overview
This skill helps product managers develop structured alignment strategies. Its core purpose: "Turn a product initiative into a structured influence plan — who needs to be aligned, in what order, and exactly what to say to each person in their language."

## Key Components

**Metadata:**
- Author: Mohit Aggarwal
- Version: 1.0.0
- Category: stakeholder-communication

**Required Inputs:**
The skill needs four foundational pieces: your initiative description, stakeholder roster with roles, timeline constraints, and any known obstacles or political context.

**The Five-Step Process:**
1. Create a stakeholder map documenting role, concerns, decision authority level, and current position
2. Map the critical conversation sequence
3. Lead discussions with stakeholder priorities, not your proposal
4. Prepare one objection response per person
5. Identify who shouldn't be approached prematurely

**Output Structure:**
Users receive a stakeholder matrix, recommended conversation order with reasoning, and customized talking points covering the person's primary concern, your specific request, anticipated pushback, counter-arguments, and success metrics.

**Key Principle:**
"Never send the same message to all stakeholders — calibrate every time." The skill emphasizes role-specific framing: engineering teams need feasibility acknowledgment first, finance requires ROI positioning upfront, and compliance functions need risk mitigation addressed initially.
