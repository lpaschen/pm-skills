# PM Weekly Review Skill — Complete Content

## Overview
This skill structures a product manager's weekly review into a focused 20-minute ritual that synthesizes metrics, shipping progress, blockers, and priorities into a shareable update. It's triggered by keywords like "weekly review," "PM standup," or "sprint health."

## Core Structure (20 minutes)
- **5 min — Metrics:** Track movement and surprises
- **5 min — Shipping:** Document shipped items, slips, and blockers
- **5 min — Insights:** Capture customer feedback and research findings
- **5 min — Priorities:** Identify three critical next-week focuses

## Key Output Sections

The template includes a standardized format featuring:

**Metrics table** tracking primary, secondary, and health metrics with week-over-week comparisons and trend indicators.

**Progress summary** organized by shipped items, in-progress work, slipped/blocked items (with owners assigned), and carry-forward tasks.

**Insights & signals** documenting "Customer feedback," "Support signals," and "Research / data."

**Next week priorities** presented as a ranked table with owner assignment and clear done criteria.

**Reflection section** (optional) for personal insights on process improvements and uncertainties.

## Critical Guidelines

- Maintain documents under 400 words for stakeholder readership
- Assign explicit owners to every blocked item
- Send by Friday end-of-day, not Monday morning
- Escalate any blocker appearing three consecutive weeks
- "Always name a clear owner for every blocked item — 'the team will figure it out' is a blocker in disguise"
