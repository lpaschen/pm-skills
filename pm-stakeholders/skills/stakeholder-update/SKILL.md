# Stakeholder Update Skill - Complete Content

This skill document provides a comprehensive framework for creating executive status updates using the BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) principle. Here are the key components:

## Core Structure

The template includes six main sections: BLUF with status indicators (🟢🟡🔴), a progress summary limited to 3-5 bullet points, a metrics dashboard showing current versus target values, risks and blockers with mitigation strategies, upcoming milestones across 30 and 90-day windows, and decisions needed when applicable.

## Key Principles

The guidance emphasizes "Lead with conclusions, not process" and focusing on impact rather than activities. For example, rather than listing interview counts, highlight the key discovery: "Identified #1 barrier to adoption (complexity of setup)."

The document stresses making "problems visible early" by proposing solutions alongside risks, and using data to tell the story through quantified metrics and trend indicators.

## Audience Adaptation

Different stakeholder groups receive tailored versions: C-Suite updates emphasize business impact and OKRs; engineering leadership receives technical context; cross-functional teams get balanced perspectives with explicit asks; board/investor communications highlight metrics, positioning, and financial implications.

## Frequency Recommendations

Daily standups use ultra-brief formats (3 bullets), weekly updates follow the full template at one page length, monthly reviews allow 2-3 pages with deeper analysis, and quarterly reviews use presentation format for comprehensive strategic discussion.

The example provided demonstrates a product team update with realistic metrics, risk documentation, and clear decision framing for stakeholder consideration.
